Notes

Retrodiction, not a track record

Most of this page is retrodiction, not a track record. LOWFI didn't exist in 2011. Every completed season here is today's model read backwards over a season that's already been played, revealed to it a week at a time - so it answers "how does LOWFI read 2011," not "what did LOWFI say during 2011." For 2023 through 2025 it's reading its own training data.

The shaded stretch at the right is 2026, and that part is live: every point is what that Tuesday's run produced, before the games.

League scoring

Average points per team per game, straight from the schedule. No model here - just what happened, for context behind the ratings above.

Best and worst

Each rating gets three readings. End of season and season average use completed seasons only - where a team finished, and how it looked across the whole year, which can be very different for a team that collapsed in December. Single week is the highest and lowest weekly estimate anywhere in the database, live weeks included.
